# TOM - Talk To Me Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

## What TOM stores

TOM is a local educational mental-health support chatbot. If you choose **I Agree** in the consent prompt, TOM stores the following information in the local `backend/chats.db` SQLite database:

- The messages you send and TOM's replies
- The time of each stored chat
- Your selected mood, detected intent, and risk level when available
- A random browser session ID
- Your consent decision and the time it was recorded

TOM does not ask for or intentionally store your name, email address, location, IP address, browser fingerprint, or device information.

## Your choice

Data collection is optional. You can use TOM after choosing **No Thanks**; in that mode, the browser uses local fallback replies and does not send chats to the backend.

If you agree, you can later select **Delete my stored data and choose consent again** in the chat screen. This permanently removes the chats and consent record linked to the current browser session, clears the local session ID, and shows the consent prompt again.

## How information is used

Stored conversations may be reviewed by the project team to evaluate and improve TOM for this educational project. TOM does not sell or share individual conversations with third parties.

## Storage and security

Data is stored locally in a SQLite database on the machine running the TOM backend. This version does not encrypt the database. Do not use TOM to share information that you would not want stored on that machine.

## Crisis support

TOM is not a replacement for professional mental-health care. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis service immediately. In the United States and Canada, call or text **988**. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number or a trusted crisis service.
